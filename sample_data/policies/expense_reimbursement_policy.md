## 1. Objective and Core Principles

This policy governs the reimbursement of authorized, ordinary, and reasonable expenses incurred by employees during legitimate business operations, client travel, and team development.

## 2. Reimbursable Categories and Spending Caps

### 2.1 Meals and Per Diem
- During approved overnight business travel, individual meals are reimbursed up to daily maximums:
  - Breakfast: up to $25
  - Lunch: up to $35
  - Dinner: up to $65
  - Maximum daily cap: $125 (USD) inclusive of taxes and standard gratuity (15-20%).
- Itemized merchant receipts are mandatory for all meal claims. Alcoholic beverages are non-reimbursable unless part of an authorized client entertainment dinner hosted by a Director or VP.

### 2.2 Transportation and Mileage
- **Commercial Flights:** Economy class is mandatory for domestic flights under six (6) hours. Premium economy or business class is permitted for international flights exceeding six (6) continuous hours with VP pre-approval.
- **Rideshare & Taxis:** Uber/Lyft/taxi fares for travel between airport, lodging, and client sites are fully reimbursable.
- **Personal Vehicle Mileage:** Reimbursed at $0.67 per mile (or local statutory equivalent).

### 2.3 Lodging and Hotels
- Standard business hotel rooms up to $220/night in standard tier cities and $320/night in high-cost metro areas (e.g., New York, San Francisco, London).

## 3. Submission Deadlines and Documentation

1. **Itemized Receipts:** Digital photos or PDFs of itemized receipts must accompany all expense lines over $15. Credit card summary slips showing only totals are not acceptable.
2. **Timeline:** Expense reports must be submitted through the finance portal within thirty (30) calendar days of expense occurrence. Claims submitted after 60 days will be rejected.
3. **Approval Flow:** Reports are routed to the direct manager, followed by Accounts Payable. Approved claims are paid via direct deposit on the next bi-weekly payroll cycle.
