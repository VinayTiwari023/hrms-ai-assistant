## 1. Purpose

This policy sets forth the operational schedule, core collaboration hours, attendance tracking methods, and expectations regarding punctuality for NovaTech Innovations team members.

## 2. Standard Working Schedule and Hours

- **Weekly Expectation:** Standard full-time employment is based on a forty (40) hour workweek, Monday through Friday.
- **Standard Daily Hours:** 8 productive working hours per day, exclusive of an unpaid 45-minute lunch break.
- **Core Collaboration Hours:** 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local team time. All team members must be online, available for standups, meetings, and synchronous messaging during core hours regardless of flexible start and end times.
- **Flexible Bands:** Team members may initiate their workday between 7:30 AM and 10:00 AM, with corresponding departure between 4:15 PM and 6:45 PM.

## 3. Daily Attendance Recording and Punch System

1. **Digital Check-In:** Employees record attendance through the NovaTech HRMS portal or automated single-sign-on (SSO) login.
2. **Punctuality & Grace Period:** A daily grace period of fifteen (15) minutes is permitted (up to 10:15 AM). Arrival after 10:15 AM without prior notification is marked as "Late Arrival".
3. **Cumulative Late Arrivals:** Three (3) recorded late arrivals within a single calendar month result in an informal notification to the employee and line manager. Five (5) or more late arrivals within a rolling quarter may initiate performance counseling.

## 4. Break Times and Rest Periods

- **Lunch Break:** One forty-five (45) minute meal interval typically between 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM.
- **Short Breaks:** Two 15-minute rest breaks are encouraged daily to prevent screen fatigue and ergonomic strain.

## 5. Overtime and Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)

- Overtime applies exclusively to non-exempt roles and requires advance written authorization from the Department Manager.
- Exempt engineering, design, and management professionals working approved weekend release cycles receive equal Compensatory Off (Comp-Off) days, to be availed within forty-five (45) days of the event.

## 6. Unplanned Absences and Notification Protocol

- If an unexpected emergency or illness prevents work, employees must notify their manager and post in their team's private channel no later than 9:30 AM on the day of absence.
