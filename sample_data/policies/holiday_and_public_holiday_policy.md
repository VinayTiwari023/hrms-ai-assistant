## 1. Policy Overview

NovaTech Innovations recognizes ten (10) statutory fixed paid corporate holidays per calendar year, supplemented by two (2) optional floating cultural/religious holidays.

## 2. Fixed Corporate Holidays (Calendar Year 2026)

The official office locations and regular operations are closed on the following paid dates:

1. **New Year's Day:** Thursday, January 1, 2026
2. **Republic / Foundation Day:** Monday, January 26, 2026
3. **Good Friday:** Friday, April 3, 2026
4. **Labor Day / May Day:** Friday, May 1, 2026
5. **Mid-Year Innovation Day:** Friday, July 3, 2026
6. **Independence Day:** Friday, August 14, 2026
7. **Autumn Equinox / Gandhi Jayanti:** Friday, October 2, 2026
8. **Festival of Lights (Diwali):** Wednesday, November 11, 2026
9. **Thanksgiving / Gratitude Day:** Thursday, November 26, 2026
10. **Christmas Day:** Friday, December 25, 2026

## 3. Floating Cultural / Religious Holidays (2 Days)

- Full-time employees receive two (2) discretionary floating holidays annually.
- Floating holidays allow celebration of individual cultural, religious, or personal milestones (such as Eid, Lunar New Year, Yom Kippur, Hanukkah, or birthdays).
- Floating holidays must be submitted in the HRMS at least one (1) week in advance and do not carry over into subsequent years.

## 4. Weekend Holiday Observance Rules

If a recognized fixed holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. If a holiday falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday.

## 5. Holiday Pay and Critical Support Coverage

Team members in 24/7 technical operations, customer support, or on-call emergency rotations who work on a recognized company holiday receive holiday premium pay (1.5x standard hourly rate) plus an alternate compensatory day off.
