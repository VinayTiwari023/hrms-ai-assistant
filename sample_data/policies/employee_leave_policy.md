## 1. Purpose and Philosophy

NovaTech Innovations believes sustained high performance requires adequate rest, rejuvenation, and work-life harmony. This policy establishes clear, fair, and transparent guidelines for all forms of time off, ensuring equitable administration across all engineering, product, sales, and operational teams.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time regular employees, part-time employees, and fixed-term contractors of NovaTech Innovations globally, subject to local statutory overrides where applicable.

## 3. Definitions

- **Annual Leave (Paid Time Off - PTO):** Planned paid days away from work for vacation, rest, and personal projects.
- **Sick Leave:** Paid time off utilized when an employee is unwell, injured, or attending scheduled medical appointments.
- **Casual / Personal Leave:** Short-notice leave for pressing personal obligations or emergencies.
- **Maternity Leave:** Statutory and company-extended leave for mothers welcoming a new child.
- **Paternity Leave:** Leave for partners welcoming a newborn or newly adopted child.
- **Bereavement Leave:** Compassionate time off following the loss of an immediate family member.
- **Accrual Period:** The calendar year from January 1 to December 31.

## 4. Leave Categories and Annual Entitlements

### 4.1 Annual Leave (20 Days per Year)
- Full-time employees accrue 1.67 days of Annual Leave per calendar month worked.
- A maximum of five (5) unused annual leave days may be rolled over into the subsequent calendar year. All rolled-over days must be utilized before March 31 of the new year, or they lapse automatically without financial encashment.
- Leave requests of three (3) consecutive business days or longer must be submitted to the direct team manager at least two (2) weeks in advance.

### 4.2 Sick Leave (12 Days per Year)
- Full-time employees receive an allotment of twelve (12) paid sick leave days on January 1 of each calendar year.
- Unused sick days do not accrue, roll over, or encash at year-end.
- For absences exceeding two (2) consecutive business days, an authorized medical practitioner's certificate must be attached upon return.

### 4.3 Casual / Emergency Leave (5 Days per Year)
- Intended for urgent, unforeseen personal commitments that cannot be scheduled outside working hours.
- May be taken as half-day increments.

### 4.4 Parental Leave
- **Maternity Leave:** Twenty-six (26) weeks of fully paid leave for birth mothers.
- **Paternity Leave:** Four (4) weeks of fully paid leave for partners, usable within the first twelve (12) months following birth or adoption.

### 4.5 Bereavement Leave
- Up to five (5) consecutive paid days for immediate family members (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- Up to three (3) consecutive paid days for extended family members (grandparents, parents-in-law).

## 5. Application and Approval Procedures

1. **Submission:** All leave applications must be logged via the NovaTech HRMS portal prior to the absence or on the day of return for sudden illness.
2. **Manager Review:** Line managers must approve or reject pending requests within two (2) business days of submission.
3. **Leave Calendar:** Approved leaves automatically sync with the team availability calendar and Slack status indicator.

## 6. Exceptions and Escalations

- In extraordinary compassionate or medical circumstances, employees may request an unpaid Leave of Absence (LOA) for up to three (3) months with written approval from the Department Head and Chief People Officer.
- Misuse of sick leave or unauthorized absence exceeding three (3) consecutive working days constitutes job abandonment and may trigger disciplinary review under the Code of Conduct.
