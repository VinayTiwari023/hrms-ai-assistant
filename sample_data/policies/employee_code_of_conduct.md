## 1. Guiding Ethical Principles

NovaTech Innovations fosters a culture built upon radical accountability, intellectual humility, unwavering integrity, and zero tolerance for discrimination or harassment. Every employee represents the organization in interactions with clients, vendors, open-source communities, and colleagues.

## 2. Workplace Harassment and Equal Opportunity

- **Anti-Harassment Stance:** NovaTech enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against any form of sexual, verbal, visual, or physical harassment, intimidation, or bullying.
- **Protected Characteristics:** Employment, advancement, and compensation decisions are strictly merit-based, irrespective of race, color, religion, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or veteran status.

## 3. Conflict of Interest and Outside Employment

- Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or familial relationships with suppliers, partners, or competitors.
- Secondary employment, freelancing, or advisory roles are permitted only if they do not involve competing technologies, utilize company equipment, or occur during company working hours, and must be pre-cleared by Legal & Compliance.

## 4. Gifts, Meals, and Anti-Bribery Standards

- Employees may never solicit gifts, favors, or entertainment from external vendors or clients.
- Unsolicited promotional gifts valued under $50 (USD) may be accepted. Any gift, travel, or hospitality exceeding $50 must be reported to the Compliance Office or declined.
- Facilitation payments or bribes of any nature are strictly illegal and result in immediate termination and legal referral.

## 5. Whistleblower Protections and Reporting Channels

- NovaTech guarantees non-retaliation for any employee who reports suspected ethical misconduct, financial irregularities, or harassment in good faith.
