## 1. Program Mission and Objectives

The NovaTech Innovations onboarding program is a structured ninety (90) day journey designed to equip every new joiner with the technical tools, cultural grounding, mentorship, and operational clarity necessary to achieve long-term career fulfillment.

## 2. Onboarding Milestones and Timelines

### Phase 1: Pre-Boarding (Offer Acceptance to Day 1)
- Digital submission of background verification documents and tax forms.
- Delivery of company-issued workstation, security badge, and onboarding welcome package.
- Setup of corporate email, Slack, and Google Workspace accounts by IT.

### Phase 2: First Week Foundation (Days 1 - 5)
- **Day 1 Orientation:** Welcome session with People Ops, benefits review, company history, and IT setup.
- **Buddy Assignment:** Every new hire is paired with an experienced peer "NovaBuddy" outside their direct reporting line to assist with informal cultural integration.
- **30-Day Goal Alignment:** First 1-on-1 with line manager to review initial learning milestones and project roadmap.

### Phase 3: Capability Building (Days 6 - 30)
- Completion of mandatory compliance training: Information Security Awareness, Anti-Harassment, and Code of Conduct.
- Access provisioning for code repositories, Jira boards, and design libraries.
- 30-day check-in conversation with People Operations.

### Phase 4: Full Contribution & Probation Review (Days 31 - 90)
- Lead initial production sprint or deliver designated project milestone.
- **60-Day Midpoint Check:** Review progress against learning objectives with line manager.
- **90-Day Formal Probation Assessment:** Comprehensive evaluation of technical competency, collaborative behavior, and core values alignment. Successful completion confirms regularized full-time status.

## 3. Managerial Responsibilities

Hiring managers must prepare an approved 30-60-90 day performance roadmap prior to the new hire's start date, schedule weekly 1-on-1 coaching sessions, and submit the formal 90-day assessment form at least five (5) business days before the conclusion of the probationary window.
