## 1. Overview and Hybrid Model

NovaTech Innovations operates under a collaborative hybrid work model designed to balance flexible autonomy with in-person team connection.

## 2. Remote Work Eligibility and Quotas

- **Standard Hybrid Entitlement:** Full-time employees who have completed their initial 90-day probationary onboarding are eligible for up to two (2) remote work days per calendar week.
- **Office Anchor Days:** Tuesday and Thursday are designated Organization Anchor Days where in-office presence is expected for cross-functional workshops, design critiques, and all-hands ceremonies.
- **Fully Remote Exceptions:** Full-time remote arrangements require approval by the Department VP, IT Security, and HR Director based on job function suitability and infrastructure readiness.

## 3. Remote Work Expectations and Security

1. **Workspace Ergonomics:** Remote employees must maintain a dedicated, distraction-free workspace equipped with high-speed internet (minimum 50 Mbps download / 15 Mbps upload).
2. **IT Equipment and VPN:** Work must be conducted exclusively on company-issued laptops. Personal computers (BYOD) are strictly prohibited for processing customer or source code assets. Company VPN and endpoint protection agents must remain active at all times.
3. **Availability:** Team members working remotely are subject to standard core hours (10:00 AM - 4:00 PM) and must be reachable via Slack and Google Meet.

## 4. Work-From-Home Stipend

- NovaTech provides a one-time home office setup reimbursement of up to $500 (USD) for ergonomic furniture, external monitors, or peripherals, subject to receipt submission within sixty (60) days of hire.
- A monthly remote connectivity stipend of $50 is added automatically to payroll for employees classified as remote or hybrid.

## 5. Revocation of WFH Privileges

Remote privileges are discretionary and may be suspended if an employee is placed on a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) or found in breach of data confidentiality.
